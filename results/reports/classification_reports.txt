FEATURE GUIDE
================================================================================

Response Delay: how quickly a node reacts to disturbances. Higher values mean slower reaction.

Demand Flexibility: how adaptable a node's demand is. Higher values mean more flexibility.

Power Balance: net generation or consumption at a node. Positive values typically mean generation dominance.
